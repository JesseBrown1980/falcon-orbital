Falcon may independently verify bytes and observations. It does not become the universal narrator,
operator apex, cosign replacement, or authority to fire another device. Trilateral evidence is a
proof topology, not a new hierarchy rank.

## False-down doctrine

```text

```

Only evidence supports the last label. “Down from here” is not “dead everywhere.”

## No-inflate companion

Independent agreement does not make all routes globally live forever. Every liveness result keeps
its host, timestamp, route, and source hash. A historical green attestation is real history, not a
permanent current-health claim.

## Merge rule

obvious to merge when conflict-free. Hold keys, personal data, device control, authority changes, or
unsupported global-liveness claims.
